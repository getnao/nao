| `page_trees`            | string[]              | No       | `[]`      | Pages whose whole subtree to sync (the page and all its descendants), as IDs or URLs                                      |
| `labels`                | string[]              | No       | `[]`      | Labels to sync; every page carrying one is pulled. Scope to a space with 'SPACE:label'                                    |
| `spaces`                | string[]              | No       | `[]`      | The space keys to sync in full, e.g. 'ENG' or 'DATA'                                                                      |
